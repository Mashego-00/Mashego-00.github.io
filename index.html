01 — About

Two disciplines, one way of thinking.

I'm in my third year of a BCom in Economic Science, majoring in Economics and Informatics at North West University. The combination isn't accidental: economics taught me to ask what the data means, and informatics taught me to build the thing that produces it.

In practice that means I've designed and normalised an Oracle database, led a team through a system-analysis project end-to-end, written advanced SQL to interrogate e-hailing operations, and estimated regression models on US house prices. I like the part where a messy problem becomes a clean schema, a clear dashboard, or a defensible number.
